# Bilingual CV refresh

## Goal
Provide a clearer, more personal, recruiter-friendly CV in English and Indonesian while preserving the existing one-page A4 format and factual claims.

## Design
- Produce two polished, ATS-friendly one-page PDFs with parallel content and hierarchy.
- Use a first-person introduction focused on the candidate's bridge between industrial systems and backend engineering.
- Make experience, selected projects, verified contribution snapshot, grouped skills, and education easy to scan.
- Retain the restrained monochrome visual identity with a single muted accent and readable sans-serif body typography.
- Link the English homepage to the English PDF and the Indonesian homepage to the Indonesian PDF.

## Content constraints
- Translate and edit existing facts only. Do not invent personal motivation, outcomes, scale, or performance figures.
- Preserve the organization contribution count and its snapshot date as stated by the source data.
- Keep private company repository names and client details out of both versions.

## Acceptance criteria
- Both PDFs are one-page A4, text-selectable, and visually checked after rendering.
- Each language reads naturally and has equivalent content.
- The existing CV download action resolves to the matching language version.
- Build and repository verification pass; both PDFs are present in the production output.
